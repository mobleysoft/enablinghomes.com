In the meantime, these are real, free, national services that can connect you to local in-home accessibility help today:

  • Eldercare Locator — the U.S. Administration for Community Living's official public directory of local Area Agencies on Aging, which can refer home-modification and accessibility help by ZIP code.
  • 211.org — a free, nationwide United Way service that connects callers to local home-safety and accessibility resources; dial 2-1-1 or search online by ZIP code.
